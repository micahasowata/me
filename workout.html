4-Day Upper / Lower Split

20 years old · 74kg · Bench 45kg · Bulking
Day 1 — Lower Body
Strength · Heavy
🔥Warm-Up
  • 5 min light cardio (jog, bike, or jump rope)
  • Leg swings – 15 each leg
  • Walking lunges – 10 each leg
  • Air squats – 15 reps
  • Specific warm-up sets for deadlift: bar × 10, 50% × 5, 70% × 3, 85% × 2
💪Main Workout
  • Deadlifts 4 × 5 Build the foundation
  • Squats 4 × 5 Deep and controlled
  • Leg Press 3 × 8
  • Step-Ups 3 × 10 Each leg
Rest 3–5 min between heavy sets
❄️Cool-Down
  • 5 min light walking or cycling
  • Hamstring stretch – 30s each side
  • Quad stretch – 30s each side
  • Glute stretch – 30s each side
  • Calf stretch – 30s each side
Day 2 — Upper Body
Strength · Heavy
🔥Warm-Up
  • 5 min light cardio
  • Arm circles – 15 each way
  • Band pull-aparts or face pulls – 15 reps
  • Push-ups – 10 reps (slow tempo)
  • Specific warm-up sets for bench: bar × 10, 50% × 5, 70% × 3, 85% × 2
💪Main Workout
  • Bench Press 4 × 6 Current: 45kg
  • Weighted Chin-Ups 4 × 6
  • Overhead Press 3 × 8
  • T-Bar Rows 3 × 8
Rest 3–5 min between heavy sets
❄️Cool-Down
  • 5 min light cardio
  • Chest stretch (doorway) – 30s each side
  • Lat stretch – 30s each side
  • Triceps stretch – 30s each side
  • Shoulder stretch – 30s each side
Day 3 — Lower Body
Hypertrophy · Moderate
🔥Warm-Up
  • 5 min light cardio
  • Leg swings – 15 each leg
  • Bodyweight squats – 15 reps
  • Hip circles – 10 each way
  • Light RDLs with empty bar – 10 reps
💪Main Workout
  • Leg Press 4 × 12
  • Romanian Deadlifts 3 × 12 Light, controlled
  • Split Squats 3 × 12 Each leg
  • Calf Raises 3 × 15
Rest 60–90 sec between sets
❄️Cool-Down
  • 5 min walking
  • Hamstring stretch – 30s each
  • Quad stretch – 30s each
  • Hip flexor stretch – 30s each
  • Foam roll quads & glutes (if available)
Day 4 — Upper Body
Hypertrophy · Moderate
🔥Warm-Up
  • 5 min light cardio
  • Arm circles – 15 each way
  • Band pull-aparts – 15 reps
  • Scapular push-ups – 10 reps
  • Light dumbbell press – 10 reps with light weight
💪Main Workout
  • Pull-Ups 3 × AMRAP Beat last week's reps
  • Push-Ups 3 × AMRAP Beat last week's reps
  • Dumbbell Press 3 × 12
  • Dumbbell Row 3 × 12 Each arm
Rest 60–90 sec between sets • AMRAP = As Many Reps As Possible
❄️Cool-Down
  • 5 min light cardio
  • Chest stretch – 30s each
  • Lat stretch – 30s each
  • Biceps stretch – 30s each
  • Triceps stretch – 30s each
  • Upper trap stretch – 30s each
📅 Weekly Schedule
Mon — Lower (Strength) Tue — Upper (Strength) Wed — Rest Thu — Lower (Hypertrophy) Fri — Upper (Hypertrophy) Sat — Rest Sun — Rest
📈 Progression
  • • Add 2.5kg to main lifts each week
  • • Add 1 rep to secondary lifts each week
  • • AMRAP sets: beat your previous rep count
  • • Leave 2–3 reps in reserve on heavy days
  • • Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on hypertrophy days
Nutrition (Bulking)
~2,800–3,000 cal • 150–180g protein • 350–400g carbs • 80–100g fat

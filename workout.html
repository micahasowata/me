Day 1 — Upper Body A

Targeted Muscles: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Abs

  • Warm-Up (5–8 minutes):

  • Light Cardio — 5 minutes

  • Arm Circles — 15 reps each direction

  • Band Pull-Aparts — 15 reps

  • Push-Ups (Slow Tempo) — 10 reps

  • Main Lifts:

  • Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Lat Pulldowns: 4 sets × 8 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Barbell Rows: 3 sets × 8 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Overhead Press: 3 sets × 8 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Accessories & Core (Triset — 3 Rounds, 60s rest between rounds):

  • Triceps Cable Pushdowns: 3 sets × 10–12 reps

  • Dumbbell Hammer Curls: 3 sets × 10–12 reps (Biceps & Forearms)

  • Cable Crunches: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

  • Cool-Down (5 minutes): Doorway chest stretch, lat stretch, triceps stretch (30s each).

Day 2 — Lower Body A

Targeted Muscles: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back, Calves, Abs

  • Warm-Up (5–8 minutes):

  • Light Cardio — 5 minutes

  • Leg Swings — 15 reps each leg

  • Air Squats — 15 reps

  • Empty Bar RDLs — 10 reps

  • Main Lifts:

  • Zercher Squats: 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs): 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Leg Press: 3 sets × 8–10 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Calves & Core:

  • Standing Calf Raises: 4 sets × 12–15 reps (Rest: 60s)

  • Cable Crunches: 3 sets × 12–15 reps (Rest: 60s)

  • Cool-Down (5 minutes): Hamstring stretch, quad stretch, glute stretch, calf stretch (30s each).

Day 3 — Upper Body B

Targeted Muscles: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Abs

  • Warm-Up (5–8 minutes):

  • Light Cardio — 5 minutes

  • Arm Circles — 15 reps each direction

  • Scapular Push-Ups — 10 reps

  • Light Dumbbell Press — 10 reps

  • Main Lifts:

  • Incline Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • T-Bar Rows: 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 sets × 8 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldowns: 3 sets × 8–10 reps (Rest: 2 min)

  • Accessories & Core (Triset — 3 Rounds, 60s rest between rounds):

  • Overhead Cable Triceps Extensions: 3 sets × 10–12 reps

  • Incline Dumbbell Curls: 3 sets × 10 reps (Biceps focus)

  • Reverse Barbell Curls: 3 sets × 12 reps (Forearm focus)

  • Cable Crunches: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

  • Cool-Down (5 minutes): Chest stretch, lat stretch, biceps stretch, upper trap stretch (30s each).

Day 4 — Lower Body B

Targeted Muscles: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back, Calves

  • Warm-Up (5–8 minutes):

  • Light Cardio — 5 minutes

  • Bodyweight Squats — 15 reps

  • Hip Circles — 10 reps each direction

  • Empty Bar RDLs — 10 reps

  • Main Lifts:

  • Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs): 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Zercher Squats: 4 sets × 6 reps (Rest: 2–3 min)

  • Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 sets × 8 reps per leg (Rest: 2 min)

  • Calves & Lower Back:

  • Seated or Standing Calf Raises: 4 sets × 10–12 reps (Rest: 60s)

  • Hyperextensions / Back Extensions: 3 sets × 12–15 reps (Rest: 60s)

  • Cool-Down (5 minutes): Hip flexor stretch, quad stretch, hamstring stretch, foam rolling.

Aside: Chin-Up & Dip Progression Protocol

To build competency in chin-ups and dips without taking away recovery from your main lifts:

  1. Option A (Warm-Up Practice): On Upper Body days, add 2 sets of 3–5 band-assisted repetitions of Chin-Ups or Dips as part of your general warm-up. Keep intensity low (3–4 reps away from failure).
  2. Option B (End-of-Workout Finisher): Right before your cool-down on Day 1 (Dips) and Day 3 (Chin-Ups), perform 1 single set to near-failure using a resistance band or weight-assisted machine.

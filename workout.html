5-Day Stamina & Physique Plan

Nutrition Baseline

  • Meal 1 (Pre-Workout): 1 cup oats, 1–2 tbsp honey, 4 scrambled eggs
  • Meal 2 (Post-Workout): 1 cup oats, 1 tbsp honey, 1 cup Greek yogurt or protein shake
  • Meal 3 (Lunch): Lean chicken breast, 1 cup white rice, mixed greens
  • Meal 4 (Dinner): Extra lean ground beef or turkey, sweet potato, broccoli

Universal Warmup & Cool Down

Warmup (5 Mins)

  • 2 mins jump rope
  • 10 arm circles
  • 10 leg swings
  • 10 bodyweight squats

Cool Down (5 Mins)

  • 2 mins slow treadmill walk
  • 30-second static stretches (quads, chest, hamstrings)

5-Day Strength & Stamina Routine

Note: Use moderate weights that leave 2–3 reps in the tank. Keep rest to 60 seconds between sets to build conditioning without adding excess muscle mass.

Day 1: Upper Body Push & Pull

  • Barbell or Dumbbell Bench Press: 4 sets of 10–12 reps
  • Barbell Bent-Over Rows: 4 sets of 10–12 reps
  • Dumbbell Overhead Press: 3 sets of 12 reps
  • Conditioning Finisher (3 Rounds):
    • 15 Kettlebell Swings
    • 45-second Plank

Day 2: HIIT & Core

  • Treadmill or Rower Intervals: 30 seconds max sprint / 30 seconds easy walk (20 total rounds)
  • Hanging Leg Raises: 3 sets of 12–15 reps
  • Cable Woodchoppers: 3 sets of 15 reps per side

Day 3: Lower Body Strength

  • Barbell Back Squats or Goblet Squats: 4 sets of 10–12 reps
  • Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs): 4 sets of 10–12 reps
  • Dumbbell Walking Lunges: 3 sets of 12 reps per leg
  • Conditioning Finisher (3 Rounds):
    • 15 Box Jumps
    • 45-second Wall Sit

Day 4: Deadlifts & Conditioning

  • Conventional Barbell Deadlifts: 4 sets of 8 reps
  • 20-Minute AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible):
    • 200m Run or Row
    • 12 Single-Arm Kettlebell Clean & Press
    • 15 Push-ups

Day 5: Full-Body Athletic Circuit

  • Dumbbell Thrusters (Squat to Overhead Press): 4 sets of 10 reps
  • Inverted TRX or Barbell Rows: 4 sets of 12 reps
  • Dumbbell Farmer’s Carries: 4 sets of 50 meters
  • Conditioning Finisher (3 Rounds):
    • 100 Jump Rope turns
    • 10 Burpees

Bulk Routine — 74kg → 90kg Lean

You: 20 y/o male, 74 kg, 188 cm (6'2") Goal: 90 kg lean (~+16 kg)

Reality check first

That's a big, long-term goal — realistically 2–4+ years of consistent training and eating, with the fastest gains in year one and a slow grind after that. Don't chase the scale week to week; chase consistency. This plan is built to support that timeline without you getting hurt or fat-only.

Nutrition targets (this is 70% of the "bulk")

Target
Estimated maintenance ~2,800–2,900 kcal/day
Starting bulk calories 3,200–3,400 kcal/day
Protein 160–180 g/day (~2.2 g/kg)
Fat ~75–90 g/day
Carbs Rest of calories (fuels lifting)
Weekly weigh-in target +0.25–0.4 kg/week

How to adjust: Weigh yourself daily, average the week. If you haven't gained after 2 straight weeks, add 150–200 kcal. If you're gaining faster than 0.5 kg/week, you're adding more fat than muscle — pull back 150–200 kcal.

Sleep 7–9 hrs. Muscle is built recovering, not just lifting.

The Split

4 days/week, compound-lift focused:

Day Focus
Mon Upper – Strength
Tue Lower – Strength
Wed Rest
Thu Upper – Volume
Fri Lower – Volume
Sat/Sun Rest (light walking is fine)

Progressive overload rule: when you hit the top of the rep range on all working sets, add weight next session (2.5kg upper body, 5kg lower body). Deload (cut weight/volume ~40%) every 6–8 weeks for a week.

Progression checklist

  • Log every session — weight, sets, reps. If you're not writing it down, you're guessing.
  • Add weight only when you hit the top rep on every set with good form.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks: same exercises, ~60% of normal weight, for one week.
  • Re-check your calorie/weight trend every 2 weeks and adjust food, not the program.
  • Expect strength (and visible size) to climb fast for the first 3–6 months, then slow — that's normal, not a sign something's wrong.

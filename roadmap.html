32-Week Roadmap

Algorithms, C++, and discrete math — discrete math readings now follow Epp (5th ed.); Lewis & Zax kept only where Epp has no match.

The Build

Phase 0 — The Launchpad Weeks 1–2

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 1 Speaking Mathematically; Ch 4 Elementary Number Theory & Proof Methods; Ch 5.1–5.4 Sequences & Induction (incl. Strong); Ch 6 Set Theory

C++: Ch 1 Basics; Ch 2 User-Defined Types; Ch 3 Modularity

Projects: Prime Tester with Proof; Summation Calculator

Neetcode: none yet — math & C++ warm-up

Phase 1 — Algorithm Analysis Weeks 3–4

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 11.1–11.2 Analysis of Algorithm Efficiency (Order Notation); Ch 5.2 Sequences & Summation Notation

C++: Ch 4 Classes; Ch 5 Essential Operations

Projects: Big-O Visualizer; Recursion vs Iteration Benchmark

Neetcode: Contains Duplicate, Valid Anagram, Two Sum

Phase 2 — Elementary Data Structures Weeks 5–7

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 6 Set Theory (revisit); Ch 9.1–9.7 Counting & Probability (Subsets, Permutations, Combinations)

C++: Ch 6 Templates; Ch 11 Containers

Projects: DIY Vector; Stack-Based RPN Calculator; Word Frequency Counter

Neetcode: Reverse Linked List, Merge Two Sorted Lists, Linked List Cycle, Remove Nth Node, Valid Parentheses, Min Stack, Evaluate RPN, Contains Duplicate II

Phase 3 — Sorting & Design Paradigms Weeks 8–10

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 5.2 Summation Notation; Ch 5.6–5.8 Recursively Defined Sequences & Solving Recurrence Relations

C++: Ch 12 Algorithms (std::sort, lambdas); Ch 14 Random Numbers

Projects: Sorting Showdown; Dutch National Flag; Binary Search on Custom Data

Neetcode: Merge Intervals, Sort Colors, Kth Largest Element, Find First/Last Position, Search Insert Position, Merge Sorted Array

Phase 4 — Trees, Heaps & Advanced Structures Weeks 11–14

Discrete Math: Epp Ch 10 Graphs & Trees; Lewis & Zax Ch 18 Coloring (no Epp equivalent, kept)

C++: Ch 7 Concepts & Generic Programming; Ch 13 Utilities (unique_ptr, span)

Projects: DIY Binary Search Tree; Autocomplete with a Trie; Priority Queue Task Scheduler

Neetcode: Invert/Max Depth/Validate BST, Level Order Traversal, LCA of BST, Max Path Sum, Construct from Preorder/Inorder, Serialize/Deserialize, Find Median from Stream, Top K Frequent, Kth Largest in Stream, Last Stone Weight, Implement Trie, Word Search II

Phase 5 — Graph Algorithms Weeks 15–18

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 8 Relations (Digraphs); Ch 10 Graphs & Trees (revisit — directed graphs, connectivity); Ch 9.8 Probability Axioms & Expected Value

C++: Ch 15 Concurrency (optional); Ch 13 std::optional

Projects: Graph Builder & Traversal; Shortest Path in a Maze; Kevin Bacon Game

Neetcode: Number of Islands, Clone Graph, Pacific Atlantic, Surrounded Regions, Course Schedule I/II, Network Delay Time, Cheapest Flights K Stops, Path with Min Effort, Connected Components, Graph Valid Tree, Redundant Connection, Word Ladder, Alien Dictionary

Phase 6 — Strings & External Memory Weeks 19–21

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 7.4 Cardinality (Countable/Uncountable Sets); Ch 12 Regular Expressions & Finite-State Automata

C++: Ch 9 Strings & Regex; Ch 10 Input/Output

Projects: Large Dictionary Autocomplete; Boyer–Moore String Search; Closest-Pair Visualizer

Neetcode: Longest Substring w/o Repeats, Longest Repeating Char Replacement, Min Window Substring, Group Anagrams, Sliding Window Maximum, Longest Substring K Distinct, Design Autocomplete System

Phase 7 — Limits of Algorithmic Power Weeks 22–25

Discrete Math: Epp Ch 2 Logic of Compound Statements (Normal Forms 2.1, Circuits 2.4–2.5); Epp Ch 4.6–4.8 Modular Arithmetic, Division Algorithm, GCD; Lewis & Zax Ch 31 Public Key Cryptography (no Epp equivalent, kept)

C++: Ch 14 Numerics; Ch 16 History & Compatibility

Projects: N-Queens Solver; Knapsack (Greedy/DP/Branch-and-Bound); RSA Key Generator; TSP Approximation

Neetcode: Subsets I/II, Combination Sum I/II, Permutations, N-Queens, Sudoku Solver, Climbing Stairs, House Robber I/II, Decode Ways, Coin Change I/II, Longest Palindromic Substring, Edit Distance, Unique Paths, Min Path Sum, LCS, Word Break I/II, Max Product Subarray, Best Time to Buy/Sell Stock

Phase 8 — Appendices & Integration Weeks 26–27

Discrete Math (Epp): Ch 5.2 Summation Notation (revisit); Ch 5.6–5.8 Recurrence Relations (revisit)

C++: Ch 11 Containers, Ch 5 Essential Operations (revisit)

Projects: Recurrence Solver

Neetcode: Median of Two Sorted Arrays, Merge k Sorted Lists, Trapping Rain Water (review)

Phase 9 — The Macro-Project Weeks 28–32

Choose one: Real-Time Route Planner · Full-Text Search Engine · Network Packet Routing Simulator · Cryptography Toolkit

Neetcode: 1–2 problems/day alongside the capstone — Hards you skipped, review Mediums, 30-min interview conditions

Beyond the Roadmap

Compiler — OCaml, via Real World OCaml. ADTs and pattern matching fit ASTs and type checkers cleanly; build it in OCaml, not just after reading about it.

Distributed KV store — Rust, via The Rust Book. Ownership formalizes the RAII discipline from Phases 2 & 4. Pair with a from-scratch Raft implementation for consensus.

Full-stack / microservices — Go for the backend via Let's Go and Let's Go Further (Alex Edwards) — routing, database layer, auth, deployment — and JS for the frontend via Eloquent JavaScript. Containers, service discovery, and message queues remain open past a single Go service.

clean the repo. commit often.
